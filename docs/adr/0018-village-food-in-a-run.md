# ADR 0018: Village food in a run

Date: 2026-10-12 · Status: Accepted · Requirements: GP-02, GP-03, GP-16 (also GP-05, GP-22, UI-01, UI-05). Amends [ADR 0010](0010-armies-orders-and-combat.md) (gathering) and [ADR 0014](0014-ownership-conquest-and-loyalty.md) (an owner's supply).

## Context

The M4 playtest reported: "gather food does not seem to work; or maybe the towns do not generate enough food to maintain stock enough to feed the army". Food on the run island is not short: it nets +351 (Lean year) to +2,342 food value a day, against 11.7 a day for a band of 13. Three causes, measured on seeds 1, 3, 4 and 5:

- **No feedback.** GatherFood is one shot: within `recruit.radius` the captain deals once and the order ends, whether or not anything moved. Only a success was logged, `Trade` logged nothing, and the captain panel's "Recent" showed the last four lines of every faction, so the player's own were pushed out. The player saw "ordered to gather food", then the order vanished and nothing followed.
- **What a gather could reach, and when.** Every dealing read and drew only the storehouse. On the run island the storehouse is a transit buffer: villagers empty it into homes and hands at dawn and dusk, and carters refill it only from about 11:00 to 17:00. Medians in days of the village's own need, days 1-2: storehouse 0.00-0.35 at 06:00 and 1.0-1.76 at 12:00, while homes held 1.05-1.64 and hands 0.4-0.7. On the first day at 06:00 the storehouse held 0, homes 0.65 and hands 1.28. A neutral band of 13, not hungry, got a median of 0 days of food per gather on days 1-2 in every seed (at least a day in 10-39% of village-hours), while the settlement panel's Food security (storehouse and homes) read 1.2-2.8 days. This was the known gap G22 in docs/m4/BUILD_GUIDE.md.
- **A raider band with nothing to do stayed forever.** After a director band raided its target, with no prey (the player's army was bigger) and no village of the player's to raid, `raiders::think` held it, Aggressive and without orders, 13-14 tiles from the village. At threat 0.9 every villager hid or fled; in all four seeds the village nearest the start produced nothing for days, its storehouse stayed at 0, and its people starved with food in their homes.

## Decision

- **Feedback on every explicit dealing (all presets, log only).** A dealing that moved food logs it with its worth in days of the army's need: "Ashford shared food with Your warband (+2.1 days)", "took food from" (given and seized), "bought food from", "supplied", "plundered". A GatherFood or Trade order (the player's or the AI's), or a told owner's supply, that moved nothing logs one reason, the first that applies: the baggage has no room for a unit of the most nourishing food ("baggage is full (4.0 of 4 days)"); a barter with no goods to trade, or goods that fetch less than a unit of the cheapest food ("too little to trade"); a plunder of an empty storehouse; the village holds no more than it keeps ("had no food to spare ... (it holds X days of food and keeps K)", over the pool the dealing reads); else its share came to less than a unit of food ("had only crumbs to spare"). A holding within 0.05 days of the keep, and a sliver of a day, print with two decimals, so no line reads "+0.0 days" or "holds 0.5 ... keeps 0.5". Automatic gathering (`forage`, every 30 ticks) stays silent when it fails. The captain panel's "Recent" shows only lines naming the captain's faction, and its Food line says how a hungry army helps itself at villages it passes, at `orders::forages_below`'s thresholds: "(hungry: helps itself)" for a neutral army under a day, "(hungry: buys food)" for a passive one, "(plunders villages it passes)" for an aggressive one under three days, and nothing for an army that never eats (a guard camp, a band for hire). `MilitaryLog` is exempt from the hash, so no outcome changes.
- **The pantry (run only).** In a run every food dealing but plunder (asking, the hungry take, passive buying and `Trade`, an owner's supply) sees the village's storehouse and homes, the food its Food security counts, and draws from the storehouse first, then the best-stocked homes (ties by SpawnId; `orders::food_sources`, `take_food`). A neutral army is given half of what the pantry holds beyond progression.ron `gather.neutral_keep_days` (0.5) of the village's need, up to the baggage's room. That keep is at least villagers.ron `loyalty.food_days` and `requests.food_days`, so a gift alone never costs loyalty or makes the village ask for food. An owner's supply keeps army.ron `garrison.keep_days` (1.0) over the pantry. Payment for a barter goes to the storehouse. Everything stays in the serial logistics step; no new state.
- **Plunder stays storehouse-only, in every preset,** so raiders and the rival plunder the player's villages exactly as before.
- **Raiders linger, then withdraw (run only).** A director band that has raided its target with nothing else to do waits director.ron `band_linger_hours` (12), then marches to a passable tile in a region (land it can reach: never water or an islet, both `NO_REGION`, as the director's spawn checks) 10 tiles beyond army.ron `threat.radius` from the nearest settlement and at least `threat.radius` from every one (`raiders::withdraw_tile`: away from that settlement, then 30, 60 ... 150 degrees either side). Prey and the player's villages keep priority, as before. Outside a run nothing changes.

## Consequences

- A band of 13 (1.75 days in its baggage) ordered to gather at 06:00 on the second day (the storehouse's morning low) at the independent village of about 120 people gets its fill: test `gather`. Seed 4 (storehouse 0.15 days, homes 1.65): 27.1 food value, 2.3 days, where the storehouse-only rule gave nothing ("holds 0.7 days of food and keeps 1.0" on arrival). Seed 3, "Lean year" (storehouse 0, homes 1.37): 27.2, 2.3 days, against 9.2 (0.8 days) before. A second gather at once says "baggage is full (4.0 of 4 days)". From the probe's samples, a band of 13 with an empty baggage gets a median of 4.0 days (the cap) per gather on days 1-2 (at least a day in 87-99% of village-hours), against 0-0.2 before; an army of 40 gets 2.7-3.8 days (Lean year 1.5). The first morning stays lean for villages under about 150 people (food is in villagers' hands); the feed line and the play guide say so.
- The pantry applies to every faction's dealings in a run: the rival's armies, supplied by its own villages, also draw on their homes. The bot plays faster (seed 1, ten days: 11 conquests against 5); `bot_run` guards the balance. The better-fed bot first lost seed 3 (4 of 5): it marched 62 mostly unarmed men straight past the rival's seat to attack a village beyond it and met the garrison. The bot now counts the seat's garrison when the march, not only the target, comes within 80 tiles of the seat (`SEAT_BERTH`), and wins 5 of 5 on days 15.5-26.3, against 17.5-30.6 before (docs/m4/PROGRESS_BOT.md).
- The playtest's own scene (a fresh neutral band of 13 with a day of food, ordered at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 on day 0 and 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 on day 1 to gather at the village nearest the start, director running; seeds 1 and 3): before, 10 of the 12 orders brought nothing and no line at all (the two that worked hit the storehouse's midday high: 2.4 and 1.0 days); after, every order brings food and a line: 2.7 and 0.7 days for the orders given at 00:00 on day 0 (the band arrives before the first dawn, when the starting stock is in villagers' hands), and 3.0-3.2 days (the baggage filled to its 4) at every other hour.
- After a neutral gather a village keeps at least 0.5 days plus half its surplus in storehouse and homes, with 0.4-0.8 days more in hands; an owned village keeps at least a day. Villages gathered from heavily may dip under `requests.food_days` at the morning low and ask for food a little more often. The hungry take (keep 0) now reaches homes: a large starving army can empty a hamlet's homes, at -0.05 goodwill.
- A raided village is back at work about 12-14 hours after the raid instead of days. Its DriveOff request then resolves after `requests.clear_hours`: done if a player captain is within 40 tiles, failed otherwise. Withdrawn bands cannot forage and may starve and desert, freeing `max_bands` slots.
- Developed and skirmish outcomes are unchanged (only the state hash moves, because progression.ron and director.ron are hashed as text); log lines of every preset gain the amount suffix and the reasons. Both data fields have serde defaults, so older saves load and adopt the new rules; replays recorded before this change don't reproduce. `SAVE_FORMAT` is unchanged.
- Tests: `gather` (integration: `a_band_of_thirteen_is_fed_on_the_second_morning`, `the_first_morning_gather_says_what_it_got`), `a_band_with_nothing_to_do_lingers_then_withdraws` (director), and the sim_military unit tests `in_a_run_a_neutral_gather_draws_on_stores_and_homes`, `a_gather_that_moves_nothing_says_why`, `trade_and_supply_say_why_too`, `plunder_takes_only_the_storehouse`, `outside_a_run_gathering_reads_only_the_storehouse`, `the_run_neutral_keep_never_costs_loyalty`, `gather_defaults_match_the_shipped_data`, `withdrawing_walks_out_of_reach_of_every_settlement`, `withdrawing_never_heads_for_an_islet` and `the_default_linger_matches_the_shipped_data`; game_ui's `recent_lines_keep_your_own`, `the_food_note_follows_posture` and `food_dealing_lines_join_the_feed`.

## Rejected

- **Counting the pantry in every preset.** It would change the developed world and the M2/M3 tests (GP-03's posture ordering is measured there), for a problem only the run island's transit storehouse has.
- **Letting plunder reach homes.** Raiders and the rival would strip the player's villages far harder than before, a balance change nobody asked for.
- **Making GatherFood retry until something moves.** A captain would stand at a village for hours with no word; a clear line and the player's own choice are better.
- **Deferred, real in every preset:** `add_output` drops output that doesn't fit full hands (12-21%); meals are sized at 0.15 per unit, so villagers eat about 25% more than they need; hiding villagers don't eat from the storehouse. Fixing any of them changes the developed world and the M2 tests.
