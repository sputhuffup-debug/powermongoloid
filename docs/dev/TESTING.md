# Tests

```sh
cargo test --workspace                                  # everything: ~9 min of tests plus the build (16 min warm, ~36 min cold on 4 cores with -j 2; bot_run is ignored)
cargo test-fast                                         # unit tests only, seconds (alias)
cargo test -p integration_tests --test posture          # one integration test file
cargo test -p integration_tests --test orders attacking # tests whose names match
cargo test ... -- --nocapture                           # see what tests print
```

The sim crates build at `opt-level = 3` even in dev, so the scenario tests run at full speed without `--release`.

## What CI runs

On every push and PR (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`):
1. `cargo fmt --check`
2. `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`
3. `cargo test --workspace`
4. `cargo doc --workspace --no-deps` with warnings as errors
5. a replay smoke run: record 10,000 ticks with 200 commands, then verify the replay in parallel and serially
6. a run replay smoke (M4): the bot plays 24,000 ticks of seed 42 (`--run --bot --record`), then the replay verifies in parallel and serially
7. the bot balance run (M4): `cargo test --release -p integration_tests --test bot_run -- --ignored` (below)

Nightly (`.github/workflows/bench.yml`): `cargo bench -p sim_headless`, compared with the previous night's results. It fails on a slowdown over 10%. `tick/run_island` (M4) measures a run at day 10: the bot plays seed 42 once to day 12, a fresh app replays its commands to day 10, and each measured tick is a plain `step(1)` (no bot cost; ~1.5 ms on 4 cores). `cargo bench -p sim_headless -- run_island` runs it alone; `sim_headless --run --bot --timings --stats-every 250` gives the worst tick per window.

## Unit tests (in the crates)

These run in seconds and sit next to the code they test.

- **sim_core:**
  - calendar phases and clock; a game hour is a 24th of a day
  - RNG stream independence
  - hash reference vectors and the coverage check
  - replay parsing (with a run's config) and divergence finding

    - `a_blow_marks_both_sides_and_ranks_change_the_damage`: one blow (the real `resolve_strikes`, run once) marks striker and victim as having fought and puts both armies in battle at that tick; an Elite striker adds exactly its rank's `attack` times the roll, an Elite victim takes exactly its rank's `defense` less; a killing blow names the striker's faction in `Casualties::slain_by`.
    - `a_battle_ends_after_a_quiet_hour_and_ranks_its_fighters`: a battle whose last blow was at tick 0 is still on at tick 99 and ends at the hourly step at 100: those who fought count a battle, `fought` is cleared, rank follows the thresholds (one at the edge of rank 2 reaches it), one who didn't fight is untouched, the `Battle` entry has the losses and the head count, a `VeteranRank` each, and the champion's battle points; with `RunState.config` cleared, battles count but nobody ranks and no points are earned.
    - `ranks_follow_battles_and_the_veteran_rate`: ranks by battles at the shipped thresholds, 25% faster with a VeteranRate of 1.25, rank 0 gives nothing, the names, and the `veterans` validation.
  - the champion (SIM-35, `progression/champion.rs`): `trait_draws_prefer_new_traits` (three distinct traits from the pool, never a held one while three new ones exist, topped up from held ones otherwise); `points_past_two_ranks_post_two_trait_offers` (a run app: points past two ranks give two `RankUp`s and two offers at the next hourly step, not before, drawn in turn from `traits ^ champion` at that tick)
  - the rival (GP-28, `rival.rs`): `the_seat_guards_only_its_own_near_it` (the seat's defence bonus covers only the rival's people within `seat_radius`, and nobody outside a run); `defence_steps_and_shipped_rows` (one valid row per difficulty, the bonus follows its steps, bad rows refused); `levies_fill_the_garrison_and_raids_split_it` (a run app: a levy of 7 enlists the rival's own villagers from several of its settlements into the garrison; a raid of 6 splits an aggressive `RivalBand` of six off it with its head-count share of the food, none lost)
  - the pacing director (GP-16, `director.rs`): `shipped_data_is_valid_and_bands_grow_by_day` (a personality per difficulty, band sizes by day, which events count as the run's first); `rejects_bad_data`; `the_default_linger_matches_the_shipped_data` (ADR 0018: the serde default of `band_linger_hours` is the shipped value, and director text without it parses to the shipped data)
  - raiders withdrawing (GP-16, ADR 0018, `raiders.rs`): `withdrawing_walks_out_of_reach_of_every_settlement` (on a flat map: a band already beyond the radius of every centre stays; one 10 tiles east of a centre goes to 10 tiles beyond the radius straight east; with water there it turns 30 degrees and stops at the shore, still beyond every radius; on a map the radius covers there is nowhere to go); `withdrawing_never_heads_for_an_islet` (seed 42's run map, which has islets: with a centre placed so that straight away from it lies a passable islet tile, outside every region, the band never picks that tile, only a tile in a region beyond the radius, or none)
  - starvation deaths (G4, `a_starved_soldier_and_captain_are_removed`): a soldier and a captain who starve to death in upkeep are despawned on the next tick and counted as lost, and a follower takes command.
  - `survey_twenty_seeds` (`#[ignore]`, run with `--include-ignored survey --nocapture`): prints each seed's start draws for tuning `progression.ron`; `SURVEY_SEEDS=42,7` picks seeds

  - detail bands and hysteresis

## Writing tests

- Prefer a **scenario test** that asserts the requirement over one that asserts implementation details. See [HOWTO.md](HOWTO.md#add-a-scenario-test).
- **Print the numbers** a test checks, so a failure in CI explains itself (`-- --nocapture` shows them locally).
- If a test needs a specific world layout, **search the generated world** for it (as `army_march` does for a pair of villages) or **edit the map** before the first tick with `WorldMap::set_terrain`, rather than hard-coding coordinates from one seed.
