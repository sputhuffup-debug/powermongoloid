# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | A person's current action (`ActionKind`), its target, its stage (travel, perform, deliver, done) and timers. The SEED.md data model calls it `CurrentAction` |
| **Allegiance** | The faction a person belongs to. Villagers follow their settlement's `Owner` (0, independent, until it changes hands); soldiers have their warlord's faction |
| **Army index** (`ArmyIndex`) | A read-only summary of every army, rebuilt each tick in Knowledge: position, flow fields, head count, food. Lets soldiers read their army in parallel |
| **Awe** | A loyalty term (`LoyaltyTerm::Awe`, `demand.awe` +0.1) a submitted town carries in the hours it is watched (GP-32, M4a step 5; `tenure::rebuild_terms`). It ends when the town settles |
| **Band** (detail band) | How a person or building is drawn at a camera distance: textured detail, flat low-poly, billboard or dot (`render.ron`) |
| **Band template** | Who follows the champion at the start of a run: groups of followers with their stats, weapons and tools, plus days of food (`bands` in `progression.ron`). One is drawn per run (GP-27) |
| **Baggage** | An army's food and goods: a `Stockpile` on the captain's entity |
| **Battle** | A fighting episode of one army (SIM-36): from the first blow a member strikes or takes (`Captain.battle_since`) until an hourly step finds none for army.ron `veterans.quiet_hours` (`last_engaged`). Its end (`veterans::end_battles`) counts a battle for every living member who struck or was struck and reports a `Battle` event with the army's losses (`battle_losses`) |
| **Bond** | A loyalty term (`LoyaltyTerm::Bond`) of a town that invited you in (GP-32, M4a step 5): `tenure.bond`, plus `kept_bond` per defence kept (`Tenure.kept`: an answered drive-off or a `Defended` deed there), at most `max_bond`; once forsaken `forsaken_after` times it is `forsaken_bond` for good (`tenure::bond_term`) |
| **Boon** | A card picked from an offer of three: the starting boon before the first tick (GP-27), dominion boons later (GP-24, see Dominion boon). Kept for the run in `RunProgress.boons` |
| **Bot** (scripted player) | `sim_headless::bot` (GP-16, GP-21): plays a run between ticks through ordinary player commands only (`--run --bot`), so its runs record and replay. It reads SpawnId-sorted queries, keeps its own notes of each captain's task, and plays to win: food, recruits, conquest, captains, then an assault on the rival's seat. The `bot_run` balance test needs it to win 3 of 5 fixed seeds within 40 days |
| **Captain** | A commander with an order queue and a posture. The player commands captains, not soldiers |
| **Captain slot** | Room for one more of the player's captains in a run (GP-25): `max(dominion.first_village_slots once a settlement is held, floor(people held / dominion.per_captain))` plus `CaptainSlots` boons and modifiers, at most `dominion.cap` (8). The champion's own army needs none; `AppointCaptain` beyond the slots is refused ("no captain slot"). Live in `sim_military::captain_slots`, stored hourly in `RunProgress.captain_slots`. Outside a run only army.ron `max_captains` applies |
| **Cause** (`Cause`) | Why something happened, carried by a `SimEvent` and kept in its chronicle entry (UI-09): the player's choice, a request, a conquest by a captain, an invitation, starvation, plunder, a revolt, a raid, holding ground, a boon, a death at a faction's hands, a request that lapsed, neglect, a fight; M4a adds a sack, a submission, a subversion, a defiance, a taking's fallout, a reprisal, an edict, an agent caught and a steward deposed (tags 15-23). Append only (the tag is hashed and saved). `Cause::describe` gives plain words for a summary line; the chronicle's sentences name the people and places through progression.ron `chronicle.causes` ([ADR 0017](../adr/0017-events-and-chronicle.md)) |
| **Champion** (`Champion`) | The player's own figure in a run: the captain of the starting band, with `Traits` and a `ChampionRank`. A marker on the person, never in `Captain`, so it stays with the person when its army passes to an heir. Its death passes the title to another captain once a run (see Succession), and otherwise ends the run in a loss (SIM-35) |
| **Champion rank** (`ChampionRank`, Rank) | The champion's rank and points (SIM-35). Deeds earn progression.ron `champion.points` (a battle its army lived through, a monument taken, a settlement conquered or invited into, a request answered); each rank in `champion.ranks` reached posts an offer of three traits at the next hourly step (`champion::rank_ups`, `RankUp`). Not to be confused with a soldier's veteran rank |
| **Battle report** (`BattleReport`) | One army's account of a battle (UI-09), written into `Chronicle::reports` when its battle ends, with its `Battle` event: the factions it fought, its dead and the foes it killed, the turning point (the first tick one of its men ran, or none if the line held) and the soldiers who rose in rank, by name. `Chronicle::report_for` finds it from the entry; `describe` tells it ([ADR 0017](../adr/0017-events-and-chronicle.md)) |
| **Chronicle** (`Chronicle`) | The run's append-only history of notable events with their causes, and a battle report per battle (UI-09). Hashed and saved; tests assert on it, never on messages. `sim_military::chronicle::describe` words an entry ("Day 3: Your warband took Saltholt Stones, by holding the ground."), with cause phrases from progression.ron `chronicle` |
| **Collector** | The nearest captain of the town's owner within `edicts.reach` tiles of its centre (`orders::collector`, GP-34, M4a step 4), by distance then SpawnId; a tithe needs one with room in its baggage. It receives the tithe's goods and enlists the Levy's mustered. No collector in reach means nothing moves that day |
| **Command** (`SimCommand`) | The only way input reaches the sim: player or AI, logged for replays if the player issued it |
| **Conquest** | Taking a settlement by force (GP-05). An `Attack` provokes the village and fights its militia. Once the defenders break, the army holds the centre uncontested for `conquest.hold_hours`, and `conquest::transfer` hands the settlement and its residents over (`SimEventKind::Conquest`, `FactionStats.conquests`). In a run, taking the rival's seat wins |
| **Courting** | Taking a town by its own wish (GP-32, M4a step 5): gifts (`Deed::Gift` from `Give`), defence (`Deed::Defended`), answered requests and influence raise its goodwill until it invites you (goodwill at least `invitations.goodwill`). Sworn towns then expect defence (see Bond, Forsaken) |
| **Cycle hold** (`haul.cycle_hold`) | In a run (SIM-64), how much more a worker's Work scores while a cycle that has used its inputs is under way, so a meal waits for its end instead of throwing the inputs and the time away. The inspector shows it as "cycle under way" |
| **Dawn line** | The feed's one line a day for what your lands sent (GP-34, M4a step 4): the `Tribute` events of the day's tithes and levies (feed only, one per receiving captain) grouped into "Day 7, dawn: your lands sent 34 food, 6 arms and 5 men to 2 bands" (`feed::dawn_line`). Later steps may add lines for terms that decided (UI-11) |
| **Deed** (`Deed`) | What moved a settlement's goodwill (GP-22): trade, equip, seize, plunder, provoke, killing, request or boon; M4a adds gift, defended, influence, whisper, festival, forsaken, threat and plot (tags 8-15). Deltas per deed are in progression.ron `goodwill_deeds` (gift, influence and whisper are computed where they happen). `Deed::spreads` is false for influence and whispers, which word of mouth never carries |
| **Deed log** (`DeedLog`) | A run settlement's last eight goodwill changes, dated (SIM-61, M4a): on day N, a faction's deed moved its goodwill by so much, `heard` when word of it came from a neighbour. Same day, faction, deed and hearsay merge. Written only by `apply_goodwill`; what the panel's "remembers" line reads ([ADR 0020](../adr/0020-lordship.md)) |
| **DecisionTrace** | The scored options behind a person's last decision, shown in the inspector. Not hashed |
| **Defended** (`Deed::Defended`) | A town saw you fight for it (GP-32, M4a step 5): a player army ended a battle with a kill against raiders or the rival, its captain within `threat.radius` of the town (independent or yours). `goodwill_deeds.defended`, at most once a town a day (`Tenure.defended_day`, also spent by an answered drive-off), never against bandits whose home is that town; at a sworn town it is a promise kept |
| **Defiance** (`Cause::Defiance`, `Defied`) | A submitted town throwing off its lord after `demand.weak_days` weak days in a row (days with fewer than `demand.watch_hours` watched hours; GP-32, M4a step 5): it goes free, told like a revolt (no champion credit), and if your soldiers stand there its militia rises against them for `garrison.revolt_hours` |
| **Dial** (`Dial`: Work, Dues, Table) | One of a run town's three edict settings, each with a `Setting` of its own (`DialState`: since, `locked_until`, and whether the player set it). The player asks with `CommandKind::Govern`; `Edicts::order_*` records the request as pending and the next hourly `govern::step` turns the dial, starts its lock and the first wave. See Edict, Lock |
| **Difficulty** (`Difficulty`) | `steady`, `chaotic` or `harsh`: the one setting in a `RunConfig`. It picks a row of progression.ron `rival` and a personality in director.ron (how early, how often and how strong the raider bands are, and which events auto-pause the game); it never changes RNG streams |
| **Director** (pacing director) | `sim_military::director` (GP-16): once a game hour in a run it measures the player's **tension** and brings raider bands (`RaidersArrived`) so that no stretch longer than `quiet_limit_hours` passes without a notable event and the run's first event comes within `first_event_within_hours`. Its personalities, one per difficulty, are in `director.ron`. A band raids the village nearest the player first, so the village asks the player to drive it off |
| **Dominion** | The player's share of a region's people, held in settlements it owns (GP-24): tallied hourly per region into `RunProgress.dominion` (`RegionTally`: total, held, threshold flags) |
| **Dominion boon** | A boon offered the first time the player's dominion of a region reaches a `dominion.thresholds` share (25%, then 50%): an `OfferKind::DominionBoon {region, step}` of three distinct Dominion boons, weighed by what the player holds there (`boons[].favour`). Once per threshold and region, even after the land is lost and retaken; kept for the run |
| **Drifter** | A villager who leaves a settlement that likes the player to join a player band passing near (GP-22): once a day in a run, a seeded few per settlement within progression.ron `drifters.radius` with goodwill at least `drifters.goodwill`, enlisted with `enlist_into` like a recruit (`DriftersJoined`) |
| **Edict** (`Edicts`, GP-34, M4a step 4) | A standing order to a town the player owns, set on three dials: Work (Balanced, Fields, Forge, Walls), Dues (Light, Tithe, Levy) and Table (Normal, Ration, Feast). Run only; the defaults are exact no-ops. Each is read at the point of use and never folded into `FactionModifiers` (ADR 0015). Data in progression.ron `edicts` |
| **Enlisted** | Marks a soldier: villager AI, movement and needs skip it, and `sim_military` drives it |
| **Envoys** | An invitation with no captain near (GP-31, M4a step 3): at dawn `influence::step` flags (`Influence.envoy`) up to `influence.max_envoys_per_day` independent towns inside your sphere (net at least `influence.envoy_net`) that like you enough to invite (counting that dawn's warmth), the highest net first; `invitations::step` posts a flagged town's invitation in the hours that follow ("sends envoys"), which spends the flag. The seat never sends them |
| **Event feed** (`EventFeed`) | The last 256 events, chronicled or not, numbered by `seq` for the UI's feed (UI-05); noisy kinds are grouped at the source (one `VeteranRank` per army and rank). Not hashed, but saved so the UI's cursor carries on. The military log's lines have a `seq` too (`MilitaryLog::since`) |
| **Exclusive system** | A system taking `&mut World`. Used for rare serial steps (commands, orders, casualties, desertion) |
| **`enlist_into`** | The one way a villager joins an army (GP-02, GP-22): `enlist` plus the shared bookkeeping (baggage capacity, the home settlement's population, `FactionStats.recruited`). Used by Recruit, drifters and request volunteers |
| **Faction** (`FactionId`) | `FactionId::INDEPENDENT` (0), `PLAYER` (1), `RAIDERS` (2, the scripted raiders), `RIVAL` (3, the rival warlord of a run) and `BEASTS` (4, monument guards); names and colours in `army.ron` |
| **FactionModifiers** | The per-faction sums of stat bonuses, plus a world row for everyone (run modifiers), with a breakdown of where each came from (GP-23). Rebuilt from scratch once a game hour by `sim_military::rebuild_modifiers` from held monuments, the player's boons, champions' traits and run modifiers; hashed. Consumers read `mult(faction, stat)` (1 + value) or `value` where the stat is used and never write it; with no bonuses every multiplier is exactly 1.0, so the developed world is unchanged |
| **Feast** | The Table dial's one-off setting (GP-34): if the town has `edicts.feast.min_food_days` of food and its cooldown has passed, it eats `food_days` of its need at once, gathers on the green for `gather_hours` (work scores times `feast.work`), and when it ends the dial returns to Normal and the next feast waits `cooldown_days`; if the town is then fed (`feast.food_days` of food) it also posts `Deed::Festival` and takes the Table loyalty term for `term_days` |
| **Fighter grid** (`FighterGrid`) | Everyone who can fight, bucketed in 8-tile cells with a bitmask of sides per cell, rebuilt each tick |
| **Flow field** | A Dijkstra cost field toward a goal over a window of the map; following it downhill walks the cheap way around obstacles. Captains walk the **goal field** |
| **Forage** (wild foraging) | A `Forage { radius }` order (SIM-38): the band stands and gathers wild food from the patches around it until its baggage is full or the land is bare. Not the automatic gathering from settlements an army passes (`orders::forage`, "foraging" in the M3 code) |
| **Follower** | A soldier attached to a captain, with a formation slot |
| **Food security** | Days of food a settlement holds at its population's eating rate |
| **Forsaken** (`Deed::Forsaken`, `Forsaken` event) | A sworn (invited) town of yours whose drive-off request lapsed (`requests::fail`; GP-32, M4a step 5): `goodwill_deeds.forsaken`, which spreads, and one more promise broken (`Tenure.broken`); at `tenure.forsaken_after` the Bond turns. Chronicled, quiet |
| **Front** | Where the player's and the rival's influence meet: towns or overlay cells whose net is under `influence.front` either way (GP-31, UI-12) |
| **Game hour** | A 24th of a day: 100 ticks (`Calendar::ticks_per_hour`). Data fields ending in `_hours` use it; the UI's clock face does not |
| **Garrison** | The owner's soldiers within `garrison.radius` of a settlement's centre. The `Garrison { settlement }` order keeps an army there; it is valid only on a settlement the issuer owns. A garrison deters revolts, and is fed by owned-village supply when the village has food to spare; a hamlet's garrison lives on its own baggage |
| **Green** | The place a town gathers: near its centre. A villager the Levy mustered waits on it, and during a feast idlers walk to it (`ai::decide`, GP-34) |
| **Guard** (guard camp, `Guard`) | An army spawned at a monument at run setup to guard it (GP-23): a raider camp (raiders without the raider AI) or beasts, larger the farther it is from the start. Every member carries `Guard { monument }` and `NoUpkeep`; the captain has no orders, so the camp holds its tile and fights whatever comes within `engage_radius`. Raiders never hunt it. Its faction holds the monument until the camp is beaten and someone else holds the ground. Villages nearby ask for it to be cleared (a clear-the-camp request) |
| **Give** | A `Give { settlement }` order (GP-22): the army hands the food its baggage holds beyond `give_keep_days` of its need to the settlement's storehouse; what arrives is `FactionStats.donated`, and the army doesn't help itself to villages' food for the next quarter day |
| **Goodwill** (`Goodwill`) | A settlement's standing with each faction, -1..1, sorted by faction (GP-22). Deeds move it through the goodwill ledger: fair trade and requests answered raise it; seizing, plundering, provoking and killing its militia lower it, and word spreads to nearby settlements. Goodwill toward the owner is the treatment term of loyalty, and seeds loyalty when the settlement changes hands |
| **Goodwill ledger** (`GoodwillLedger`) | This tick's goodwill changes (`GoodwillChange`: settlement, faction, delta, deed), posted by deeds from serial steps and applied with their spread, sorted, by `apply_goodwill` first in `AgentSet::Standing`. Hashed |
| **Heartland** | A town of yours deep in your sphere: your influence share leads the rival's by at least `influence.sphere`. Its Contest term is `+influence.heartland` (GP-31; your towns only: the rival's towns deep in its own sphere get no bonus, and the panel's "the rival's heartland" only says where they lie) |
| **Holder** (`Holder`) | Who holds a monument (GP-23), on every monument in a run: the faction (0 for nobody), since when, a contender standing there alone, and the last hour the holder's soldiers stood there. One faction's soldiers alone within `monuments.hold_radius` for `monuments.hold_hours` take it; a holder long absent and far from its lands loses it to nobody (drift). Hashed |
| **Influence** (`Influence`) | What a run town's holdings push on it (GP-31, M4a step 3): each held town projects power from its people and loyalty only (`influence::power`, less its scar, the rival's seat twice), falling off linearly to 0 at `influence.reach` tiles; at every town `influence::field_at` turns the pulls into the player's and the rival's shares against the town's own independence, and the net is the player's share minus the rival's. Rebuilt hourly by `influence::step` (slot 13) and at a taking. It warms towns you don't own (`Deed::Influence`, no spread), brings envoys, sets the Contest loyalty term at owned towns, eases drifters in your sphere and, by people held, ranks the champion (`Feat::Realm`). The seat is never swayed |
| **Input delivery** | A haul from the storehouse to a workplace of an input its recipe takes (ore and wood to a smithy, grain to a bakery). In a run (SIM-64) its pickup is capped at economy.ron `haul.input_target` cycles' worth minus the stock (`act::input_cap`), and a carter takes none that another carter or a worker is already on, keeping its own (`ai::RunFixes`) |
| **Input gate** (`InputGate`) | A render-side resource (`game_render`) that blocks camera and overlay keys while a full-screen UI screen is up; game_ui also gates its hotkeys and world clicks with the `playing` run condition (M4 step 10) |
| **Invitation** | A settlement offering the player lordship (GP-22): an `Invitation` offer (accept or decline) posted hourly in a run when its goodwill toward the player passes `invitations.goodwill`, a player captain is near and its owner is weak. Accepting transfers it without a fight (`Cause::Invitation`, `Invited`), with loyalty seeded from goodwill; answering or letting it lapse starts a cooldown |
| **Levy** | Two things. The rival's recruiting (GP-28): every `levy_every_days` it enlists `levy_size` villagers of its own settlements into the seat's garrison with `enlist_into`, one per settlement in SpawnId order, round and round, and reports `RivalLevy`. Levied men walk to the seat. The Dues dial's Levy setting (GP-34, M4a step 4): each day, and in the hour you set it, `edicts.levy.per_day` of the town's people are mustered (see Muster) and a collector within reach enlists them; its Dues term is `levy.term`, or `harsh_term` at a conquered or sacked town (none at one that was invited) |
| **Load hook** | A function a plugin registers with `AppSaveExt::on_load`, run after a save is loaded (GP-12): it rebuilds what isn't saved, such as the spatial grid, and empties caches and per-tick scratch |
| **Logistics step** | `orders::run_orders`: the serial step where captains deal with settlements (recruit, food, arms, trade) |
| **Lock** (a dial's) | A dial that has just turned cannot be changed for `edicts.lock_days` days (`DialState.locked_until`). `govern::refusal` refuses a command on a locked dial ("locked"); the panel greys the dial with that reason and the days left (`govern::lock_left`). A new request on a pending dial replaces it |
| **Lordship** (M4a) | The milestone that turns towns from flags into places ([LORDSHIP_DESIGN.md](../LORDSHIP_DESIGN.md), [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-lordship.md)): ways to take a town that leave a lasting tenure, itemised loyalty terms and a dated deed log, influence, fallout (bandits and reprisals), edicts, stewards, building orders and specialised towns. Everything is run-only; progress slots 13-16 drive it |
| **Loyalty** (`Loyalty`, `SettlementLoyalty`) | A villager's loyalty to its settlement's owner, 0..1 (GP-06, SIM-24). Once a day it drifts toward a target set by food security, goodwill toward the owner and time under the owner (`loyalty` in villagers.ron); independent settlements aim at a fixed level. `SettlementLoyalty` holds each settlement's mean and minimum, and `below_since`, when its mean fell below the revolt threshold (unrest) |
| **Loyalty term** (`LoyaltyTerm`, `LoyaltyTerms`) | One itemised pull on a run settlement's loyalty target (SIM-60, M4a): Resentment, Awe, Contest, Bond, Dues, Table, Steward, Stirred. Rebuilt hourly by `tenure::step` (slot 14), which sums the terms other modules expose (`influence::contest_term`, `govern::dues_term` ...); their sum is `loyalty_target`'s `bias`, added before the clamp. Absent outside a run (bias exactly 0.0). Terms are named in the panel's "why" |
| **Militia** | Villagers whose AI chose `Fight` to defend a provoked settlement (stance `Militia { against }`) |
| **Monument** (`Monument`) | A landmark the run preset's worldgen places in every region (GP-23): standing stones, a ruined fort, a holy well, a road marker, an ancient forge, a market cross, a wayshrine or a watchtower, on a site that suits its kind and away from settlements. A sim entity with a `SpawnId` and a `Holder`; holding one gives its holder a small, capped bonus (progression.ron `monuments.kinds`) |
| **Morale** | 0..1. Low morale routs, and unfed or routed men may desert |
| **Muster** (`Mustered`) | A villager the Levy has called up (GP-34): never works, waits on the green until a collector within reach enlists him (`enlist_into`), and is sent back to work if the Levy is lifted or the town lost. A wave takes `edicts.levy.per_day` of the people (rounded, at least one), and at most `cap` wait at once. Enlisting by any means drops the mark |
| **NoUpkeep** | A marker on soldiers who never get hungry and never eat: monument guards (GP-23). `soldier_upkeep` leaves their hunger alone and `eat` skips them |
| **Occupation** | A villager's trade (farmer, fisher, ...), tied to a workplace kind |
| **Offer** (`Offers`) | A choice waiting for the player: the starting boon, a dominion boon, a trait, an invitation, a band for hire. Posted with `post_offer` inside a tick; run setup queues them quietly and the first tick reports them. Answered with a recorded `ChooseOffer` command, so picks replay (GP-27) |
| **Overlay** (`Overlay`, `OverlayMode`) | A terrain tint around settlements or monuments, cycled with O: ownership, goodwill and loyalty toward you, food security, monument holders (with rings at their hold radius) and, since M4a, influence (UI-12): your colour to the rival's from a coarse grid of the same field the sim acts on, an amber front where the shares meet, dashed edges at the sphere (net +/-`influence.sphere`) and rings round the free towns being warmed that fill toward an invitation. Recomputed twice a second (every two seconds for influence); terrain chunks rebuild only when a quantised colour changes (`game_render::overlay`) |
| **Owner** (`Owner`) | The faction holding a settlement and since when (GP-05): `FactionId::INDEPENDENT` until conquest or invitation. Changed only by `sim_agents::set_owner`, which also sets the residents' allegiance and loyalty and calls off their militia |
| **Pantry** | A settlement's storehouse plus its homes: the food its Food security counts. In a run, what an army's food dealings other than plunder read and draw on, storehouse first, then the best-stocked homes (`orders::food_sources`, [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-village-food-in-a-run.md)); outside a run, and for plunder, only the storehouse |
| **Posture** | Passive, neutral or aggressive. How an army treats villages: buy, ask, or take and frighten |
| **Preset** (`WorldPreset`) | One of the two complete settings blocks in `worldgen.ron`: `developed` (the 2048-tile M1-M3 world; `WorldGenSettings::default_data()`) or `run` (the 1024-tile run island) |
| **Progress driver** | `progression::progress`: the one exclusive, hourly system in `MilitarySet::Progress` that runs settlement- and run-level steps in a fixed order (guide D7). Its slots, in order: `veterans::end_battles`, `monuments::hold`, `conquest::revolts`, `wild::regrow`, `drifters::draw`, `requests::step`, `invitations::step`, `dominion::tally`, `champion::rank_ups`, `rebuild_modifiers` (then `dominion::refresh_slots`), `rival::clock`, `director::step`, then M4a's run-only slots 13 `influence::step`, 14 `tenure::step`, 15 `govern::step` and 16 `fallout::step` (M4c's `saga::settle` will be 17) |
| **Pull** (`Pull`, `loyalty_pulls`) | One reason a run settlement's loyalty target falls short (UI-11, M4a): hunger (the food weight left unmet), ill will (goodwill toward the owner below neutral) or a negative loyalty term. `loyalty_pulls` lists them largest first; the first two are what a revolt would cite. `deciding_term` names the term whose removal alone would carry the target across the revolt line (Awe or Bond holding a town, Resentment tipping it). Both are pure functions in sim_agents `loyalty.rs`; the settlement panel's "Why they feel this way" prints them |
| **Rally field** | A flow field toward the captain, over the army's extent. Followers far from their place walk it |
| **Raiders** (`Raider`) | The scripted hostile bands of `FactionId::RAIDERS` (M3; `raiders::think` in `SimInputSet::Ai`): fed, they hunt the nearest enemy army; hungry, they raid the nearest village. In a run they prey on the player alone (its armies no bigger than the band, its settlements), and the pacing director's bands first raid the village it aimed them at (`Raider.target`, hashed); with nothing else to do after that raid they linger director.ron `band_linger_hours` by the village, then withdraw out of every settlement's reach (ADR 0018). Guard camps are raiders without the `Raider` marker |
| **Raiding band** (`RivalBand`) | A band split off the rival's garrison on its clock (GP-28; `orders::split_army`, so raids weaken the seat). Steered by `rival::think` every 300 ticks: it attacks the player's nearest settlement or stands at the player's nearest monument, else hunts the player's armies |
| **Ration** (`Rationed`) | The Table dial's setting that slows hunger and work (GP-34): while it holds, the town and each resident carry `Rationed { hunger, work }`, copied from `edicts.ration` when it starts. Hunger rises `hunger` times as fast, a day's need counts `hunger` times (so food security stays honest) and work cycles take `work` times as long. Soldiers are swept clear of it |
| **Region** (`RegionId`) | A part of the island: settlements are grouped around farthest-apart seeds and every land tile takes the region of the nearest settlement over land. Region 0 is always the central one; count them with `WorldMap::region_count()`. A region edge borders water or another region |
| **Replay** | A seed, scenario and the player's commands, plus expected hashes (RON). Re-running it must reproduce every hash |
| **Request** (`RequestBoard`) | A settlement asking the player for help (GP-22): drive off a hostile army near it, or bring food when its food security stays low (clear a guarded camp comes with monuments). Posted hourly in a run, at most one per settlement, with a deadline and a reward from its real stocks; the sim detects completion (the food delivered by `Give`, the danger gone with a player captain there), pays the nearest player captain and raises goodwill. Hashed |
