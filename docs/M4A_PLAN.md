# M4a Plan: Lordship

Status: **planned 2026-10-13, revised the same day after a review against the code, awaiting your go.** M4a starts after the M4 playtest's first verdict (`docs/M4_PLAYTEST.md`) and comes before M4c "Saga" (`docs/M4C_PLAN.md`) and M4b "Living towns".
Design: `docs/LORDSHIP_DESIGN.md` (draft 2026-10-13; your decisions are recorded there). Spec: `docs/SEED.md` § "M4a: Lordship". Decisions: [ADR 0020](adr/0020-lordship.md) (proposed).

M4a answers the playtest: "the game feels like we are running around taking points like king of the hill but my actions feel unweighted." It turns towns from flags into places. How you take a town (conquer or sack, demand its submission, court it, or turn it with an agent) leaves a lasting tenure that its loyalty shows line by line. Your holdings radiate influence that warms free towns until they invite you and unsettles the rival's border. Bloody conquests loose bands of the town's own broken men on the rival's lands, and sometimes yours, and the rival answers with reprisals against your newest holding. Owned towns take edicts that move real villagers (to the forge, the fields, the walls, the green) within the hour, send food, arms and men to your passing armies, are run by a steward of their own people whose habits set the dials you leave alone (sometimes against you, always shown ahead and overridable), and can be ordered one building at a time. The smithy chain is repaired so a forge town really arms your men. The rival's seat is no longer an island: its defence falls as you take its realm apart.

**Value comes early, and the milestone splits cleanly:**
- **Part 1, "Take and hold"** (steps 1–6, about 59%): every town explains itself (step 1), weapons exist (step 2), your sphere grows on the map (step 3), the dials move people (step 4), three ways to take a town (step 5) and the fallout, with the seat that follows its realm (step 6). A 15-minute smoke playtest at merge point 2 (Demand, the sack, edicts and the overlay) decides whether part 2 starts; part 1 ends with a fuller playtest of its own: by then the complaint can be judged.
- **Part 2, "Stewards and works"** (steps 7–11, about 41%): subversion, building orders, stewards and specialised towns, the bot and balance pass, docs and the full playtest.
- **Built in parallel lanes** (section "Execution plan"): two sim lanes, a UI and render lane and a bot, tests and docs lane, merged at seven points, each with one full suite and `bot_run`.
- **Every step is run-only,** with two inert exceptions that reach every preset: `Veteran.trade` is written in `enlist` everywhere and read only for stewards (state hashes change in all presets; behaviour and stdout do not), and `recruit_candidates` and `would_be_militia` gain `Without<Agent>` filters that match nothing without agents. Developed, skirmish and `--battle` stdout stay byte-identical, so M4c's invariance baselines are unaffected.
- **Steps that change how a run plays, by design,** are gated by `bot_run`: 2 (weapons), 3 (influence invitations), 5 (new takes and their terms), 6 (bandits and reprisals), 7 (agents), 8 (buildings) and 9 (stewards set dials; specialties reshape populate).

## What stays unchanged

- **Stdout of the guarded commands.** Each step compares these release commands with the M4 binary's saved output (step 1 records it), and the stdout must be byte-identical. State hashes and timings, on stderr, may change.
  - developed: `sim_headless --seed 42 --ticks 4801 --stats-every 600`
  - skirmish: `--skirmish --seed 42 --ticks 6000 --stats-every 2000`
  - battle: `--battle 1000 --seed 42 --ticks 3000 --stats-every 600`
- **The M2 and M3 tests** pass unchanged, with today's tuning: `village_day`, `famine`, `army_march`, `posture`, `starving_army` and `orders`. Every Lordship rule, the smithy fixes included, is run-only, and every new component is inserted by `setup_run`; the two inert exceptions (`Veteran.trade` and the `Without<Agent>` filters) change state hashes, never behaviour, and step 1 re-records any golden hash.
- **The run-preset tests** (`champion`, `chronicle`, `conquest`, `director`, `dominion`, `gather`, `invitation`, `monuments`, `rival`, `run_start`, `save_load`, `sellswords`, `wanderers`) can move once influence (step 3), new takes (step 5), fallout (step 6), stewards and specialties (step 9) act in every run. Each is re-run in those steps. Where one moves only because the run took another path, it opts out through data it already loads: `influence.enabled`, `fallout.enabled`, `stewards.habits` (all false), an empty `specialties` list, or `haul.run_fixes: false`. `rival` also covers the realm share: at share 1.0 the seat's bonus is exactly today's. `conquest` and `invitation` guard loyalty drift: they run with Lordship on, and if the new terms move the drift they guard, the scenario's food changes, never the data.
- **`FactionModifiers` is untouched.** Per-town effects live in their own components.
- **`populate`'s draws** (`docs/m4/BUILD_GUIDE.md` G10): specialties change how many workers each trade gets, never how many RNG draws are made (checked in step 9). They do change how many workplaces each town builds, so the run island's layout and SpawnIds shift, which G10 accepts in the run preset only; the developed world and the skirmish never take the branch.
- **Replays.** `REPLAY_FORMAT` 6 from step 1, so older replays are refused with the existing message. In every step a fresh run replay records and verifies, multi- and single-threaded.
- **Saves.** `SAVE_FORMAT` 2 from step 1, **bumped exactly once**: M4 saves are refused. Every saved layout lands in step 1's skeleton; a later change to one inside M4a amends the type in place (M4a has no released saves to protect between its own steps), so M4c's `SAVE_FORMAT` is one above M4a's final value, 3 as planned. If part 2 is split off to follow M4c's part 1 and then changes a saved layout, it bumps again and says so.
- **Pacing.** Seven new notable kinds (Submitted, Defied, Subverted, BanditsRose, ReprisalVowed, RivalReprisal, AgentCaught) reset the director's quiet clock, as real events should; `bot_run`'s quiet-limit check still runs over every seed. Bandits don't count toward the director's `max_bands`.
- **M4c's plan** stays valid with its renumbering (ADR 0021, `SAVE_FORMAT` one above M4a's final value, 3 as planned, slot 17, baselines on the M4a build), done in the design commit.

## The gate (every step)

1. **Build and lint:** `cargo fmt --check`; `cargo clippy -j 2 --workspace -- -D warnings`; `cargo test -j 2 --workspace` (at a merge point; a lane step runs the unit tests of the crates it touched, its own integration tests and the guarded ones below); `RUSTDOCFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo doc -j 2 --workspace --no-deps`.
2. **Determinism:** the determinism tests, plus a run replay: record `--run --seed 42 --ticks 3000 --commands 50 --record run.ron`, then verify it with `--replay run.ron` and `--replay run.ron --single-threaded`.
3. **Invariance:** the three stdout comparisons above.
4. **Cost, for sim steps (NFR-11), on live ticks only:** `--run --seed 7 --difficulty steady --ticks 7200 --timings --stats-every 2400`, three runs, against `docs/m4a/baseline.md`, after checking stderr has no `run ended` line before tick 7,200 (if it has, compare up to the last full window before the end, and say so). That botless run owns nothing and sets no dial, so it sees only the rival's influence and the smithy fixes; **from step 4, also a bot run** (`--run --bot --seed 42 --ticks 96000 --timings --stats-every 250`, averaging the windows before the win) with Lordship's own stages (slots 13 to 16, `orders::lands`, `construction::advance`, `jobs::reassign`) summed and kept under 5% of the mean tick.
5. **Docs in the same commit** (CLAUDE.md "Developer handbook"): `docs/dev/DATA.md` for data, `docs/dev/ARCHITECTURE.md` for systems and slots, `docs/dev/TESTING.md` for tests, `docs/dev/GLOSSARY.md` for terms, `docs/dev/HOWTO.md` for recipes others will repeat, and ADR 0020's "as built".
6. **Balance, at merge points after steps marked bot_run:** `cargo test --release -j 2 -p integration_tests --test bot_run -- --ignored` still wins 3 of 5 and holds the win-day floor (no seed before day 14, the median within four days of the M4 baseline's). Record in the step notes per seed: the win day, takes by way, defiances, Forsaken deeds, weapons made and tithed, men levied, bandit bands and their raids on rival and player towns, reprisals and retakes, revolts. Change data when the game is unbalanced and the bot when it plays badly (HOWTO "Tune run balance").
7. **Frames, for render steps (3, 8):** screenshots on the dev box (lavapipe under Xvfb).

Step notes go in `docs/m4a/`, as M4's went in `docs/m4/`.

### Keeping lanes from colliding

- **Step 1 lands the shared surface,** so later lanes add bodies in their own files instead of editing the same enums: every appended variant with its tag, every type with its fields, the four slot functions, every data block with its defaults, and the words for every new kind and cause.
- **Shared files** (`crates/sim_core/src/events.rs` and `command.rs`, `crates/sim_military/src/orders.rs` and `progression/mod.rs`, the `lib.rs` and `save.rs` registrations, `assets/data/progression.ron`, `docs/dev/DATA.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `TESTING.md` and `GLOSSARY.md`) get small, local edits after MP0, rebased by the merge lead. Docs rows go in each lane's own subsection and are merged in order.
- **One owner per hot file:** lane D owns `bot.rs`; lane B owns `orders::lands` and the `Govern` arm of `orders::apply`, lane A every other new code in `orders.rs`; lane C owns all of `game_ui` and `game_render`. **One owner per term:** `tenure::step` (lane A) sums the loyalty terms, but each lane writes its own: `govern.rs` and `influence.rs` expose their contributions (Dues, Table, Steward; Contest) as functions, so no lane edits another's rules.
- **Only the merge lead** sets `SAVE_FORMAT` (2, once, in step 1) and `REPLAY_FORMAT`, and records baselines.
- **Every lane runs the fast gate per step** (build and lint with `-j 2`, its crates' unit tests, its own integration tests, the determinism tests, the three stdout comparisons and docs). The full workspace suite, the cost check and `bot_run` run once per merge point, on the merged branch.

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapons swing the balance: armed armies of +8 attack crush the seat early, or the rival's seat arms too | High | Measured at step 2 before any edict exists; the Forge cap, the input target and ore bound it; win days recorded, not only wins; data before the bot |
| Influence and envoys snowball: act 3 is skipped | Medium | Square-root power, saturating shares, a 0.6 goodwill cap from influence alone, one envoy a day, the seat immune; `bot_run`'s win days; a playtest question |
| Bandits plus reprisals overwhelm the bot (or the player) | Medium | Two bands, one vow every two days, the next scheduled raid pushed back; bandits aim mostly at the rival; the bot's newest-holding guard; data first |
| Bandits made of villagers starve and desert in a day, so the fallout feels empty | Low | Two days' food; desertion walks them home, which is its own visible story; tuned with `fallout.rs`'s counts |
| Governing is optional: stewards do it all, so nothing you click matters | Medium | A steward is a personality, not an optimiser: each trait is a visible habit that is sometimes against your interest (a Stern steward levies a sullen town, a Forge-wise one forges a hungry one), dials no habit touches stay at their defaults, the pending habit is shown a dawn ahead with an Override, and `overrides_change_outcomes` plus a playtest question ask whether overriding mattered |
| Too many dials: micromanagement | Medium | Three dials, stewards' habits for the ones they touch, two-day locks, one Dawn line a day |
| The take verbs still start by marching to a point, and the climax is one point: king of the hill again | High | Design section 1 states which verbs are army-at-a-point; the Demand watch is about holding land (a garrisoned neighbour or your sphere is enough); influence, envoys, agents, edicts and stewards need no army; the seat's defence follows its realm; the smoke playtest at merge point 2 judges the mix before part 2 starts |
| Weapons, tithes and envoys make runs trivial, and a floor-only `bot_run` would not notice | High | `bot_run` gains a win-day floor (no seed before day 14, the median within four days of M4's); `bot_uses_lordship` counts what the bot chose; the Attack-only comparison proves choices matter |
| The smithy fixes shift run food (carters busier with inputs) | Medium | Food stays first in hauling (×3 urgency); input deliveries capped; run towns' food security recorded at step 2 |
| The new loyalty terms break `conquest` and `invitation`'s drift | Medium | The terms are run-only, but those are run-preset scenarios: adjust their food, never the data; re-run at steps 1, 3 and 5 |
| Lanes collide in shared files | Medium | The step 1 skeleton; one owner per hot file; small local edits after MP0; the merge lead rebases at seven merge points |
| Rendering new kinds and sites (`Changed`, despawn) is new ground | Low | Step 8 adds both with screenshots; `parts()` is an exhaustive match, so a missing model fails to compile |
| Save and replay churn while M4c is planned | Low | M4c's plan is renumbered in the design commit; each format is bumped exactly once, in step 1, by the merge lead |
| Scope delays M4c | Medium | The split after step 6: part 1 is playable and testable alone; step 8 (the first part-2 work that depends on the verdict) waits for the merge-point-2 smoke playtest, so nothing of part 2 is sunk when part 1 is judged; if part 2 is split off it follows M4c's part 1 |
